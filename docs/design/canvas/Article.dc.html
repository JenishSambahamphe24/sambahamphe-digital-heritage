The Mundhum and the origin of the Sambahamphe clan name

What the oral scriptures say about where our name comes from, and how elders in Tehrathum still tell it.

AS [Author name] · Archive committee ·9 Kartik 2083·8 min read

[Example article text — the real article is supplied by its author.] Every Limbu clan carries its story in its name. For the Sambahamphe families, that story was told aloud long before anyone wrote it down: at weddings, at funerals and in the long evenings of Chasok Tangnam.

Over the past year the archive committee recorded elders in four villages telling the story in their own words. Their versions differ in the details, and we have kept every version rather than choosing one.

What the elders told us

Most accounts begin with an ancestor who crossed the hills and settled above a river. The name, several elders said, was given to the place before it was given to the family.

“We say the name when we bless a child, so the child knows which hill it belongs to.”

The recordings are kept in the archive. Members can listen to them on the profiles of the ancestors they mention.
