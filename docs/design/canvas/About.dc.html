About us

A community keeping its story together

Sambahamphe Utthan Sangh brings together the Sambahamphe families of the Limbu (Yakthung) people, in Nepal and abroad, to preserve our history, keep our family line and pass our culture to the next generation.

Our seal

The seal carries the Sangh's name in Devanagari and in the Sirijanga script of the Limbu language, around three symbols:

The mountain
The Himalaya of the eastern hills, the land our ancestors came from.
The Silam Sakma
The woven diamond of Yakthung culture, framing what we protect.
The diyo
A lamp kept burning: knowledge handed from one generation to the next.

The year at its foot, २०५२, is the year the Sangh was founded.
