Life

Jit Bahadur was the elder son of Senehang and grew up on the family land above the Tamor river. He farmed millet and maize on terraces his grandfather Hangrup had cut, and played the chyabrung at weddings and at Chasok Tangnam across the neighbouring villages.

A land record of 1955 BS names him as holder of the family's upper fields. His son Bhim Bahadur later moved to Dharan and was among the families who founded the Sangh.
