Chasok Tangnam celebration 2083

The harvest offering, with chyabrung, songs and a shared meal. Families from every gaau committee are welcome.

About the day

Chasok Tangnam is when Limbu families offer the first of the harvest. This year the central board and the Dharan gaau committee host it together at the community hall.

Wear traditional dress if you can. Bring a dish to share; rice and tea are provided.
